## 1. Proposed Competency

**Proposed competency statement**

> Demonstrate the ability to recognize and respond to observable conservation-area conditions through appropriate field stewardship action, documented evidence, and traceable reporting.

**Proposed canonical owner:** GeCCO-5 CAMPRServ

**Canonical descriptor:** Conservation Area Management & Public Relations Services

**Primary competency domain:** Conservation-area management and public relations services

**Supporting units:**

- GeCCO-3 SEEDS
- GeCCO-4 SMARTCOMM
- GeCCO-7 SURPLUS

## 2. Exact Authority Boundary

This document is a **governance proposal**, not a competency approval.

It does not create:

- a canonical competency ID;
- a production TCI;
- a certification credential;
- evaluator authority;
- licensing authority;
- external regulatory approval; or
- a certification decision.

The proposed competency becomes authoritative only after the authorized GeCCO governance process approves it and assigns the canonical identifier/version.

## 3. Evidence Basis

The proposal is grounded in the actual RG-003-0004 field record:

- **Location:** OCELOT Pathfinder LEAF Camp-0
- **Subject:** Monkey Falls Creek Clean up
- **Conditions:** Intermittent rain showers
- **Observed action:** cleanup of two pickup trucks full of inorganic trash
- **Evidence classes:** plastics, aluminum cans, glass bottles
- **Corroboration:** two contemporaneous photographs and one contemporaneous cleanup video
- **Human review:** Dr. Cristopher J. Cayetano
- **Review state:** Reviewed

The evidence lifecycle has already been tested through capture, persistence, human/GIS validation, reviewed-state lock, export, clear/restore, and reconstruction.

## 4. Descriptor Traceability

The proposed competency is assigned to CAMPRServ because its operational core is conservation-area field stewardship and site-condition response.

The supporting relationships do not transfer ownership:

- **SEEDS:** environmental/sustainability context.
- **SMARTCOMM:** observation, GIS/data, evidence, provenance, digital infrastructure.
- **SURPLUS:** waste/resource/material handling.

The evidence does not establish an engineering, general logistics, or technical-data competency as the primary competency demonstrated by the cleanup.

**Observable criterion → Conditions → Evidence → Pass standard**

- remediation rules;

## 7. Minimum Credible Evidence Proposal

The proposed minimum evidence for this competency should be reviewed by CAMPRServ governance.

Candidate minimum:

- direct observation or authorized practical demonstration;
- structured field record;
- sufficient documentary or physical evidence;
- provenance;
- authorized evaluator verification.

Photographs/video should be treated as corroborating evidence when appropriate, not automatically mandatory for every demonstration.

## 10. CAPABILITY ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY

**EVIDENCE**

**AUTHORITY**

The repository cannot approve its own competency.
