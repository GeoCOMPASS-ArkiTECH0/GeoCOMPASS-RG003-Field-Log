## 7. Evidence architecture

This record preserves the following distinction:

**GEOGRAPHIC REFERENCE ≠ FIELD EVIDENCE ≠ HUMAN VALIDATION ≠ AUTHORITY**

And, consistent with the established GeoCOMPASS architecture:

**CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY ≠ EVIDENCE**

Public GIS can establish geographic context and independently mapped features. It does not by itself certify the field action, establish competency, authorize an evaluator, or make a certification decision.
