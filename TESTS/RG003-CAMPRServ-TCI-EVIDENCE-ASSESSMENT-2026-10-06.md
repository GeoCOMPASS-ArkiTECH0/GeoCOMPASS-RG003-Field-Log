## Red-Team Findings

### Finding 1 — The evidence is stronger than the current governance layer.

The field proof is unusually complete for a first real lifecycle test: structured record, photographs, video, provenance, human review, export/restore, and reconstruction are all demonstrated.

The bottleneck is therefore no longer evidence capture.

**Bottleneck: governance formalization.**

### Finding 2 — Do not convert the candidate TCI into a certification rule prematurely.

The actual cleanup proves performance. It does not prove that the selected response was the only or formally correct response.

The architecture must preserve that distinction.

### Finding 3 — Additional evidence is not the bottleneck.

The minimum credible evidence principle applies. More photographs or larger video files should not be required simply because they are available.

The next proof value comes from approved criteria, evaluator authority, and a formal pass condition.
