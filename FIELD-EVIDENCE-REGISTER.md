## Validation loop

**Biosphere → Expedition → Observation → Evidence → Repository → Human/GIS Validation → Knowledge → Public Interface**

RG-003 therefore tests three distinct dimensions:

- **CAPABILITY:** Can the field-log system capture, preserve, export, and present expeditionary records?
- **EVIDENCE:** Does the record contain primary artifacts supporting the observation?
- **AUTHORITY:** Can the responsible human/GIS lead validate the relationship between the evidence and the claimed site?

**CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY ≠ EVIDENCE**
