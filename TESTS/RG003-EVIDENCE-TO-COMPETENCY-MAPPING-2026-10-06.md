## TCI Readiness

The evidence is sufficient to draft observable TCI language, but **no TCI ID or certification threshold is invented**.

A defensible future TCI for the environmental-stewardship candidate would need to specify:

**Observable performance:** perform and document a field cleanup response to identified inorganic waste.

**Conditions:** authorized field site, actual field conditions, defined safety requirements, and approved cleanup procedure.

**Required standard:** the candidate must correctly identify the waste condition, perform the authorized response, document the action, and preserve traceable evidence.

**Minimum credible evidence:** direct field observation plus field record and sufficient physical/documentary evidence.

**Pass condition:** all required observable criteria are met and an authorized evaluator verifies the evidence.

The current RG-003 record satisfies much of the evidence side of this structure, but the approved competency definition, TCI version, pass condition, evaluator authorization, and certification decision are not present.
