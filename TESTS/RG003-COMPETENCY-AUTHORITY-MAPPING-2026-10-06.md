## Authority Decision

**Rationale:** The demonstrated capability is best characterized as conservation-area field stewardship and site-condition response. This is more directly traceable to Conservation Area Management than to environmental engineering, technical evidence management, or general logistics.

### Supporting units

- **GeCCO-3 SEEDS:** environmental/sustainability context and potential eco-integrative deployment.
- **GeCCO-4 SMARTCOMM:** field observation, evidence, metrics, GIS/data, communications, provenance, and digital record support.
- **GeCCO-7 SURPLUS:** waste/resource handling and material-management support.

## Important Boundary

This is an **authority-mapping test result**, not a production certification assignment.

The test establishes a provisional owner based on the currently approved descriptor matrix.

It does **not** create:

- a new canonical competency ID;
- a production TCI;
- a certification credential;
- a licensing authority;
- an external regulatory approval; or
- authority for the field reviewer beyond the review actually performed.

Production competency ownership remains subject to the formal competency registry and authorized GeCCO governance.

## Assessment Boundary

The actual evidence is sufficient to demonstrate that the candidate performed and documented the observed field activity.

It is not sufficient, by itself, to certify a broader conservation-management competency unless the approved TCI requires no additional performance.

A future assessment should therefore distinguish:

1. **Observed cleanup performance**
2. **Evidence/documentation quality**
3. **Site-condition interpretation**
4. **Appropriateness of response**
5. **Safety/compliance**
6. **Reporting and follow-through**

The present proof strongly covers 1 and 2, provides evidence for 3, and provides a proposed next action for 6. Items 4 and 5 require an approved standard before they can be treated as certification criteria.
