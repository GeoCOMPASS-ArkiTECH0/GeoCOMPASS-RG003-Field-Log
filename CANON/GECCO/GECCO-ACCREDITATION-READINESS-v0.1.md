# GeCCO Accreditation Readiness v0.1

## Purpose

Build GeCCO so future Florida and federal recognition, authorization, accreditation, approval, licensing, or credential-recognition processes can be pursued from an organized evidence base rather than reconstructed later.

**This document establishes readiness architecture. It does not claim that GeCCO is accredited, licensed, approved, or recognized by any external authority.**

## Governing Principle

**Build to the evidence requirements now; pursue the appropriate external recognition when scope, institution, and program maturity justify it.**

## Accreditation Traceability Spine

Every credential pathway should be traceable through:

**CANON → COMPETENCY → TCI → CERTWORX → ASSESSMENT → EVIDENCE → KPI → CERTIFICATION → AUTHORITY MAPPING → RECORD → VERIFICATION**

## Authority Mapping Record

Each unit/credential should eventually identify:

- Authority or recognizing body
- Jurisdiction
- Recognition type
- Applicable law, regulation, standard, or occupational framework
- Scope of recognition
- Required institutional conditions
- Required instructor/evaluator qualifications
- Required curriculum elements
- Required assessment elements
- Required evidence
- Required records-retention period
- Required accessibility/civil-rights controls
- Required reporting/audit controls
- Review date
- Responsible GeCCO authority
- Status: mapped / under review / verified / not applicable

## Florida Readiness

Screen each GeCCO unit for potentially applicable Florida requirements, including:

- institutional authorization or education-provider requirements;
- career/workforce education requirements where applicable;
- occupational or professional licensing boundaries;
- state credential-recognition requirements;
- applicable health, safety, environmental, construction, food, water, or other regulatory requirements;
- accessibility requirements;
- records and privacy requirements; and
- state audit/reporting requirements.

The mapping must distinguish between an **internal GeCCO credential** and a credential that carries external Florida recognition.

## Federal Readiness

Screen each unit for potentially applicable federal requirements or standards, including:

- U.S. Department of Education requirements where applicable;
- federal financial-aid requirements if ever pursued;
- federal civil-rights and accessibility requirements;
- federal workforce-development requirements where applicable;
- occupational/professional standards under federal jurisdiction;
- federal environmental, health, safety, or public-sector requirements where applicable; and
- federal audit/recordkeeping requirements.

No federal accreditation or recognition should ever be implied unless formally established.

## Quality-System Evidence

The GeCCO system should naturally generate evidence useful to future external review:

- approved curriculum versions;
- competency definitions;
- TCI definitions;
- assessment rubrics;
- evaluator qualifications;
- evaluator calibration records;
- candidate progression;
- attendance/participation where applicable;
- assessment results;
- evidence records;
- remediation records;
- appeals/reviews;
- certification decisions;
- credential status;
- KPI results;
- curriculum-review records;
- policy approvals;
- version history;
- audit trails; and
- corrective-action records.

## External-Recognition Safety Rule

GeCCO may describe its own credentials accurately, but must not represent an internal credential as:

- state-approved;
- federally accredited;
- professionally licensed;
- occupationally recognized; or
- equivalent to an external credential

unless the applicable authority has formally granted that status.

## Accreditation-Readiness Acceptance Test

- [ ] Each unit has an authority-mapping field.
- [ ] Applicable Florida requirements have been screened.
- [ ] Applicable federal requirements have been screened.
- [ ] External credential/licensure boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Curriculum versions are preserved.
- [ ] Assessment standards are preserved.
- [ ] Evaluator qualifications are recorded.
- [ ] Assessment evidence is retained appropriately.
- [ ] Certification decisions are auditable.
- [ ] KPI records are reproducible.
- [ ] Appeals/reviews are documented.
- [ ] Role separation is documented.
- [ ] Data minimization/privacy controls are documented.
- [ ] Accessibility requirements are considered.
- [ ] Records-retention requirements are mapped before external submission.
- [ ] Public credential verification exposes only authorized information.

## Fulcrum Rule

Do not build a separate accreditation bureaucracy. Make legitimate GeCCO operations generate structured evidence that can serve training, quality assurance, audit, and future external-recognition purposes simultaneously.
